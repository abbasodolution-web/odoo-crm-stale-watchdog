Stale Opportunity Watchdog

Find the deals that went quiet, before they are lost

Every day the Watchdog checks your open opportunities for real activity: a note, an email, a completed activity or a stage change. When a deal has had none for too long, it is flagged in the pipeline and its salesperson gets a follow-up reminder. If nobody acts, the team leader hears about it.

Installation

Install in 3 steps

1
Get the app
Click Download on this page (free).
2
Add it to Odoo
Unzip it into your custom add-ons folder (a folder listed in addons_path) and restart Odoo. On Odoo.sh, push the folder to your repository instead.
3
Install it
Turn on developer mode, open Apps › Update Apps List, search Stale Opportunity Watchdog and click Activate.
How to use

Set it up and use it

1
Choose your limits
CRM › Configuration › Settings › Stale Opportunity Watchdog: days before an opportunity is stale, days before escalation, tags to ignore.
2
Give a stage its own limit (optional)
Open the stage and set Stale after (days).
3
Work the badges
Quiet deals show a Stale · days badge in the pipeline and a reminder for the salesperson. Log a note, send an email or move the stage and it clears.
4
Pause on purpose
Waiting for the customer? Use Pause on the deal, with a date and a reason.
5
Check the report
CRM › Reporting: revenue at risk per salesperson and stage.
FAQ

Good to know

Does it work on Odoo Online (SaaS)?
No. Odoo Online does not allow apps from the store. It works on Odoo.sh and on your own server, Community or Enterprise.
Can you change it for my company?
Yes. Write to me with what you need and I will send you a quote.
What counts as activity?
A note, an email, a completed activity or a stage change. Automatic updates do not hide a quiet deal.
