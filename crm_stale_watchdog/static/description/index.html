Stale Opportunity Watchdog

Find the deals that went quiet, before they are lost

Every day the Watchdog checks your open opportunities for real activity: a note, an email, a completed activity or a stage change. When a deal has had none for too long, it is flagged in the pipeline and its salesperson gets a follow-up reminder. If nobody acts, the team leader hears about it.

How it looks

A "Stale · days" badge on every quiet deal in the pipeline

The form says how long the deal has been quiet, with a Pause button

Pause the reminders until a date, with the reason in the chatter

Revenue at risk per salesperson and stage

Settings per company: stale after, escalation, tags to ignore

What it does

  • Real activity, not "last modified": automatic updates never hide a quiet deal.
  • A limit per stage: e.g. 7 days in Proposition, 30 days in New, with a company default.
  • "Stale · 12d" badge on pipeline cards, a Days Inactive column and a banner on the form.
  • Clears itself the moment someone logs a note, sends an email, completes an activity or moves the stage.
  • Pause reminders on a deal that is waiting on purpose, until a date, with the reason in the chatter.
  • Escalation: when the reminder stays overdue, the sales team leader gets an activity too.
  • No salesperson? The team leader gets the reminder instead.
  • Ignore tags: leave long-cycle deals out.
  • Stale Opportunities report: revenue at risk per salesperson and stage (graph, pivot, list).
  • Settings per company. Covered by 15 automated tests.

Setup

CRM › Configuration › Settings › Stale Opportunity Watchdog: days before an opportunity is stale, days before escalation, tags to ignore. To give a stage its own limit, open the stage and set Stale after (days).
